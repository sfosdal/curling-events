curling.events

About Us

curling.events is a free calendar of curling in the United States: the championships, the bonspiels, and the learn-to-curls where most curlers start.

Why curling

Curling is a small sport here, and a growing one. There is no national league to follow and no home team with a stadium schedule. The season lives in a few hundred clubs, many of them run by volunteers, and in the bonspiels they host for one another, with USA Curling’s playdowns and the world championships above them. Each club keeps its own calendar, so finding out what is on, or where to try it, means checking dozens of them. This calendar does that checking once a day and puts the answer on one page.

What you’ll find

The national championships and their qualifiers, the world championships and the Grand Slam of Curling; bonspiels by region; and the open houses, learn-to-curls, clinics and courses that clubs offer newcomers. Clubs finds the clubs near you.

Who we are

curling.events is made by Ghostwood Sites, part of Ghostwood Labs, and is one of the here.events calendars. We build and run it ourselves. There are no accounts and no ads that follow you, and nothing is sold here — every listing links to the club or organiser that runs it.
