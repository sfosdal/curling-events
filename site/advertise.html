Curling Events

Reach the curlers heading to the next bonspiel, playdown or Slam — on the calendar they check before they sign up.

Who you reach

Curling Events is a free, ad-light calendar of the curling season: USA Curling's national championships and qualifiers, World Curling's championships, the Grand Slam of Curling, and the bonspiels at Granite, Evergreen and clubs across the country, refreshed daily from the organisers' own listings. Its readers are club curlers, juniors and fans planning a weekend on the ice — the audience a club, an equipment maker, a hotel near the rink or a travel promotion wants to meet before entries close.

Placements

  • Presenting sponsor — a single line under the masthead on every page, with your mark and link.
  • Neighborhood spotlight — a card in the event list for your venue’s own nights, styled like the listings and marked as sponsored.
  • Game-day partner — your message beside the championships during Nationals and the Slams, when the page is busiest.
  • Calendar feed — a short line in the subscribe feeds that readers add to their own calendars.

How it works

Placements are sold by the month, with a minimum of one month. Creative is a short line of text and a link, or a small mark; we keep the look of the site, so there are no banners, pop-ups or tracking beyond a click count. Rates depend on placement and season — the bonspiel season and the championships set the traffic — and are quoted on request.
